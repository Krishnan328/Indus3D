# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in the Indus3D community to be welcoming, respectful, and useful to people of every background and level of 3D-printing experience.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent and give actionable feedback.
- Focus technical discussion on evidence, safety, and reproducibility.
- Respect privacy: do not share another person's printer address, camera feed, logs, or personal information.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualised language, and sustained disruption of community discussion are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact method listed in the repository profile. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with discretion. Responses may include a warning, removal of content, temporary restrictions, or a permanent ban from project spaces.

This Code of Conduct is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant.
